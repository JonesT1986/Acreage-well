Getting a well drilled near Parry Sound

A registered driller will typically run a test hole, log the aquifer, set casing and screen at the right depth, then complete the well with a pump, pressure system, and a pitless adapter that brings water below the frost line into your home. Depth and water quality vary property to property, so a local operator who knows the Ontario ground is worth more than a lowest-per-foot quote.

What shapes a well on your property

  • Depth & geology — the biggest cost driver, and it varies property to property across Ontario.
  • Water quality — iron, hardness, or sulphur are common on rural water and may call for treatment.
  • Completion — casing, screen, pump, pressure tank, and a pitless adapter below the frost line.

Common questions

Do I need a permit to drill a private well near Parry Sound?

Private domestic wells — water for your household, yard, and livestock — are usually treated more lightly than commercial or large agricultural use, which more often needs provincial approval. Ontario sets its own rules, so confirm current requirements with the provincial water regulator; a registered local driller will know the process.

How do I know a driller is legitimate?

Most provinces register or license drilling rigs and require a well record after the work. Ask your driller whether their rig is registered in Ontario and get your well record — that's the concrete, verifiable credential.

How much will a well cost near Parry Sound?

It depends on depth and geology, which vary property to property. A driller can give you a realistic depth range from nearby well records before the rig arrives.
